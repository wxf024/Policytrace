HOW TO READ THIS

Evidence before verdict.

01

Fact

A date, law, statistic or documented action supported by a primary source.

02

Official finding

A conclusion from a court, statutory inquiry, inspectorate or commissioned review.

03

Inference

A proposed relationship between events. It must be labelled and supported, not smuggled in as fact.

04

Opinion

Public or editorial judgement. Kept separate from the underlying record.
ABOUT POLICYTRACE

A political timeline, not a guilt-by-association engine.

This prototype separates policy actions, measured outcomes, investigations and causal claims. It is designed so strong conclusions can be made when evidence supports them — and visibly withheld when it does not.

Prototype dataset updated 9 August 2026. Sources are linked on every record.
